## What is the name of this street?

The challenge requires determining the name of the street shown in the street-level photograph.

Observing the overall street scene on a clear day, the urban architecture exhibits a mixture of modern glass-paneled office blocks and older classical buildings crowned with dome roofs in the background. A row of vehicles is parked along the sidewalk, where the black hatchback positioned closest to the camera provides an immediate identifying lead

![11. Name that street!.png](./images/11.%20Name%20that%20street%21.png)

Zooming in on the rear of the Mercedes hatchback reveals a standard long European license plate with a blue left band displaying country code D, accompanied by registration characters OF LC 471 and two inspection seals positioned in the middle

![11. Name that street!-1.png](./images/11.%20Name%20that%20street%21-1.png)

Comparing this structure against European vehicle registration patterns documented on Geohints confirms a standard German license plate format. Under the German system, the initial letters represent the district or city prefix known as the Unterscheidungszeichen. The prefix OF denotes the city of Offenbach am Main in the state of Hesse, while the license plate holder advertises the dealership website kunzmann.de, a known automotive dealership group operating extensively throughout this metropolitan area

While the vehicle is registered in Offenbach am Main, drivers frequently commute across adjacent municipalities within the wider Frankfurt Rhine-Main metropolitan area. To isolate the exact street, we look for permanent commercial storefronts along the roadway. Further down the sidewalk beneath the trees on the left stands a commercial storefront featuring an illuminated sign reading SHISHANDI alongside political campaign posters mounted on a lamppost

![11. Name that street!-2.png](./images/11.%20Name%20that%20street%21-2.png)

The campaign posters on the lamppost include the slogan Lindner kommt featuring politician Christian Lindner of the German Free Democratic Party FDP, corroborating that the photo captures an election cycle in Germany. Meanwhile the signage shishandis indicates an active local hookah lounge and bar

Running a targeted Google search for the business name query shishandis in Germany returns business listings across the country

![11. Name that street!-3.png](./images/11.%20Name%20that%20street%21-3.png)

The primary result points directly to Shishandis Shisha & Bar located at Stiftstraße 18 - 20 in downtown Frankfurt am Main, situated right across the municipal boundary from Offenbach am Main. The road curve, office building facades, and adjacent architecture match the challenge perspective

> Stiftstraße
