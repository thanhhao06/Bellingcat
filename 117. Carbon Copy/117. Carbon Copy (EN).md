## What words are visible on the building to the right, when viewed from the same perspective?

The challenge requires determining the words visible on the building to the right when observed from the same real-world viewpoint as the pencil sketch.

Direct reverse searches using Google Images or Yandex on the pencil sketch yield generalized architectural drawings without matching a specific historical site. Progressing requires extracting specific geometric and functional characteristics of the tower to construct domain-specific search strings

Analyzing the drawing indicates a multi-story Brick Gothic tower with a polygonal faceted floor plan, a steep gabled roof with half-hip overhangs, and timbered dormer levels near the ridge. Below the tower sits a timber sluice bridge spanning a river where an angler fishes, signifying a medieval hydraulic waterworks, water mill, or weir installation along a waterway

![117. Carbon Copy-6.png](./images/117.%20Carbon%20Copy-6.png)

Pairing these architectural identifiers in search queries such as medieval waterworks brick tower on Google locates a historic color painting depicting a brick tower sharing the identical roofline, dormer profile, and apex finial

![117. Carbon Copy-4.png](./images/117.%20Carbon%20Copy-4.png)

The color painting offers clearer material textures and contrast compared to pencil line art. Running the painting through Google Lens traces the historical structure to the Abtswasserkunst waterworks tower situated in the town of Lüneburg, Lower Saxony, Germany

![117. Carbon Copy-5.png](./images/117.%20Carbon%20Copy-5.png)

Searching for Abtswasserkunst on Google Maps at b. d. Abtsmühle 1 in Lüneburg confirms that the masonry tower still stands along the Ilmenau River, even though the timber outbuilding depicted in the historical sketch was altered over time

![117. Carbon Copy-7.png](./images/117.%20Carbon%20Copy-7.png)

Comparing the rooftop apex between the pencil sketch and the modern structure confirms an exact match with the distinctive metallic finial spear and ornamental sphere

![117. Carbon Copy-8.png](./images/117.%20Carbon%20Copy-8.png)

Exploring the 3D terrain model on Google Earth establishes the spatial relationship between the tower, the Ilmenau River, and the pedestrian bridge spanning the weir. The drawing perspective matches the view looking northeast from Brausebrücke

![117. Carbon Copy-9.png](./images/117.%20Carbon%20Copy-9.png)

Dropping into a street-level photosphere on Brausebrücke replicates the original drawing perspective, aligning the window distribution of the brick tower with the riverbank. The structure immediately to the right is a red brick commercial building belonging to a hotel complex

![117. Carbon Copy.png](./images/117.%20Carbon%20Copy.png)

Zooming in on the red brick facade directly beside the glass bay reveals metal lettering mounted on the wall reading HOTEL Bergström
