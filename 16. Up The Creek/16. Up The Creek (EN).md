## What is the name of the river in the photograph? (don't include "River" in your answer)

The challenge requires identifying the name of the winding river captured in the aerial photograph, excluding the word River.

In digital forensics and OSINT investigations, examining EXIF metadata remains the primary operational step whenever working with an original image file. Inspecting the file with exiftool reveals that the recording device retained complete geotags captured by a Pixel 6 Pro camera at coordinates 47 deg 41' 39.33" N, 121 deg 59' 41.77" W on July 9, 2023

![16. Up The Creek.png](./images/16.%20Up%20The%20Creek.png)

Plotting these coordinates onto satellite mapping brings us to a rural river valley situated within King County, Washington. To verify that the aerial perspective reflects this coordinate origin, we cross-reference ground structures and geological landmarks visible across the valley floor

In the lower-left sector of the aerial frame, we observe an agricultural compound consisting of barn outbuildings, a curving access road, and a dark pond with lily pads and surface vegetation

![16. Up The Creek-2.png](./images/16.%20Up%20The%20Creek-2.png)

Across on the opposite bank facing north lies an extensive farming property divided into a rectangular grid of cultivated vegetable beds aligned along a main roadway

![16. Up The Creek-3.png](./images/16.%20Up%20The%20Creek-3.png)

Loading satellite imagery on Google Maps at the extracted GPS coordinates and looking northward corroborates both landmark zones, aligning the farmstead beside the pond with the cultivated plots cradled within the river bends

![16. Up The Creek-4.png](./images/16.%20Up%20The%20Creek-4.png)

Zooming into the local geographic layer confirms that the waterway flowing past the rural community of Novelty and bordering Two Mountains Farm and Pepper Retriever Grounds is the Snoqualmie River

![16. Up The Creek-1.png](./images/16.%20Up%20The%20Creek-1.png)

Omitting the word River as stipulated by the challenge prompt yields the target waterway name

> Snoqualmie
