## What is the name of this body of water?

The challenge requires identifying the specific alpine lake captured in the landscape photograph.

The photograph captures an alpine lake with calm, clear waters reflecting evergreen forest canopies and golden autumn aspen foliage. Towering above the tree line in the background is a sheer granite peak characterized by a sharp, asymmetrical skyline ridge. Cropping the distinctive mountain summit and ridge line enables reverse visual search via Yandex

![18. Alpine Adventure.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure.png)

Yandex image recognition matches the geological ridge silhouette against multiple landscape photography entries cataloging national parks in the United States

![18. Alpine Adventure-2.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure-2.png)

Reviewing the matched source images identifies the mountain as Hallett Peak reaching an elevation of 3875 meters along the Continental Divide within Rocky Mountain National Park, Colorado

To narrow down which alpine lake provided the viewpoint, we evaluate local topography and visitor accessibility. Checking Google Street View coverage and trail density across Rocky Mountain National Park reveals that the western slopes are rugged, remote, and sparsely tracked, whereas the eastern slope near Estes Park hosts a dense network of paved scenic roads, trailheads, and visitor photospheres

![18. Alpine Adventure-6.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure-6.png)

Based on the direct line of sight toward the eastern face of the granite cliff and the presence of fall aspen foliage among pines, the vantage point belongs to the glacial basin east of Hallett Peak

![18. Alpine Adventure-4.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure-4.png)

Surveying the natural glacial tarns and lakes distributed across this eastern basin yields seven potential candidates, comprising Sprague Lake, Bear Lake, Emerald Lake, Dream Lake, Nymph Lake, Lake Haiyaha, and Mills Lake

![18. Alpine Adventure-3.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure-3.png)

Re-examining the skyline in the challenge photo indicates that Hallett Peak is flanked by two prominent saddle notches where the mountain wall drops into tributary valleys

![18. Alpine Adventure-9.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure-9.png)

Correlating this ridge profile with a 3D digital elevation model of the terrain shows Hallett Peak situated directly between two major glacial gorges, namely Tyndall Gorge labeled 1 to the north and Chaos Canyon labeled 2 to the south, while Glacier Gorge labeled 3 lies further south. Looking west from the lower eastern valley, these gaps align with the valley entries of gorges 1 and 2

![18. Alpine Adventure-10.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure-10.png)

Projecting a line of sight directly toward both gorges at an intermediate viewing distance capable of framing the summit reflection leads straight to the lake terminal at Bear Lake Trailhead

![18. Alpine Adventure-11.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure-11.png)

Opening a user-submitted 360-degree photosphere from the shoreline of Bear Lake looking west-southwest replicates the peak profile, shoreline rocks, and valley backdrop

![18. Alpine Adventure-5.png](./images/18.%20Alpine%20Adventure-5.png)

> Bear Lake
