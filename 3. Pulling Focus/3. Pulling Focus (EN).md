## What is the function of this building?

The challenge requires determining the operational function of the building featured in the challenge photo.

The prompt photo was taken from quite a distance and looks slightly blurry. The first details catching the eye are two flags flying above the building roof and on a pole behind it, along with a large entrance sign situated in front of the gate

![3. Pulling Focus-4.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus-4.png)

Due to the distant perspective, finer details on the flag cannot be seen clearly, only revealing a red background with a circular yellow emblem in the center. Because this flag design is uncommon, we use Geohints to filter national flags featuring a red field and a central yellow pattern, narrowing the most probable country down to Kyrgyzstan

![3. Pulling Focus-1.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus-1.png)

The next clue is the lettering on the sign in front of the gate. Although the exact language is not yet known, the blocky characters look very much like the Cyrillic alphabet widely used across Central Asia or Eastern Europe

![3. Pulling Focus-5.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus-5.png)

Cropping the sign and running it through Google Lens to recognize characters and search for information

![3. Pulling Focus-3.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus-3.png)

The system recognizes the text **КАЗАН КУЙГАН АЙЫЛЫ**. In the Kyrgyz language, the word Ayily translates to village or rural community, forming the place name Kazan-Kuygan village. Searching this name on the map locates a small settlement nestled in a high mountain valley within Naryn District, Naryn Region, Kyrgyzstan

![3. Pulling Focus-2.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus-2.png)

Observing the surrounding landscape in the original photo, the building stands right next to a major asphalt highway, backed by dry barren mountain slopes, with residential houses scattered across the valley basin below. Comparing this with the map of Kazan-Kuygan, the sole major road traversing this area is highway A365. Based on mountain alignment and residential settlement layout, the viewpoint is on the left shoulder of highway A365 looking toward the village

![3. Pulling Focus-6.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus-6.png)

Because highway A365 is the only route in the region with Street View coverage, we trace along the highway to find a perspective matching both the mountain ridges and the residential clusters, isolating the bend that leads into the village junction

![3. Pulling Focus-7.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus-7.png)

Dropping Street View at this three-way junction with imagery recorded in October 2015, we immediately spot the `КАЗАН КУЙГАН АЙЫЛЫ` signpost and the mountain silhouette matching the challenge image. However, looking at the open ground behind the signpost, the yellow building is nowhere to be found. There are two possibilities: either the structure was demolished, or it had not yet been built when Google drove through in 2015

![3. Pulling Focus-8.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus-8.png)

To find out, we inspect points of interest marked on Google Maps directly around this fork. An unexpected POI marker appears under the name **Biblioteka**, which means library in Russian and Kyrgyz

![3. Pulling Focus.png](./images/3.%20Pulling%20Focus.png)

Clicking into the user-contributed photos under Biblioteka, an image uploaded by user miro gster in December 2023 captures the newly constructed yellow building taken from a vehicle driving along highway A365, matching the challenge photo completely
