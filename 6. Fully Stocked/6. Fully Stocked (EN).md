## In which city would you find this port?

The challenge requires identifying the city where the container port depicted in the photograph is located.

Taking an overall look at the prompt image, we see an active commercial harbor with stacked shipping containers and maritime gantry cranes lining the quay. In the distance across the water, a prominent cable-stayed bridge stands out with tall structural pylons and sweeping elevated access ramps. This distinctive engineering feature serves as the most identifiable visual anchor in the entire scene

![6. Fully Stocked.png](./images/6.%20Fully%20Stocked.png)

Cropping the bridge and running a reverse image search on Google Lens returns numerous matching photos pointing directly to the Köhlbrandbrücke, an iconic bridge spanning the Köhlbrand river branch in the port of Hamburg, Germany. However, to ensure this is not simply a visually similar bridge elsewhere, we need secondary verification from within the terminal itself.

Looking closely at the red ship-to-shore gantry cranes stationed along the dock, large white capital letters are visible along the crane horizontal boom. While the beginning of the text is obscured by the angle, the trailing letters clearly spell out RDKAI

![6. Fully Stocked-4.png](./images/6.%20Fully%20Stocked-4.png)

Combining the location keyword Hamburg with the suffix RDKAI and the term ship to shore into Google, the search immediately identifies the primary container facility as HHLA Container Terminal Burchardkai, where the terminal name Burchardkai perfectly matches the letters RDKAI

![6. Fully Stocked-1.png](./images/6.%20Fully%20Stocked-1.png)

Searching HHLA Container Terminal Burchardkai on Google Maps locates the terminal on Waltershofer Damm. Browsing user-contributed photos taken on site, the viewpoint looking across the water basin directly faces the Köhlbrandbrücke, matching the exact perspective in the challenge photo

![6. Fully Stocked-3.png](./images/6.%20Fully%20Stocked-3.png)

Reviewing additional media footage and ground photos from Burchardkai confirms the exact red container cranes bearing the prominent Burchardkai lettering operating by the water

![6. Fully Stocked-2.png](./images/6.%20Fully%20Stocked-2.png)

Every piece of evidence, from the distinctive bridge silhouette to the specific terminal name on the gantry cranes, confirms that this port is situated in the city of Hamburg.

> Hamburg
